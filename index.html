01

Research

Selected threads across efficient models and systems.

A

Efficient multimodal models

With the Tambe Lab, I studied visual-token redundancy in vision-language models and evaluated selection, merging, and cluster-level aggregation methods for reducing multimodal inference cost.

Thierry Tambe · Stanford · NVIDIA Research

B

Relational foundation models

I contributed to RelBench v2, a large-scale benchmark and repository for learning directly over interconnected relational tables, including new datasets, autocomplete tasks, and unified evaluation infrastructure.

Jure Leskovec · Stanford STAR

C

Diffusion language models

I work on block diffusion architectures and systems for long-context language modeling. Our hybrid design confines reverse Mamba computation to the active denoising block, enabling exact reusable prefix caches without giving up local bidirectional mixing.

Model architecture · Training systems · Long context

D

Multilevel cache replacement

My UT Austin honors thesis, advised by Calvin Lin, studied coordinated cache replacement across multiple hierarchy levels. I implemented and evaluated replacement policies in ChampSim against classical and oracle-inspired baselines.

Calvin Lin · UT Austin · Computer architecture
