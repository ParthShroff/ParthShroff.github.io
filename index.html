Research

Selected threads across efficient models and systems.

A

Multimodal token compression

With the Tambe Lab and NVIDIA Research, I studied how redundant visual tokens inflate vision-language model inference cost. Our CVPR 2025 eLVM work systematically compares token selection, merging, and cluster-level aggregation, showing that simple cluster aggregation can outperform prior encoder-level and attention-based approaches.

I also explored Matryoshka-style variable-length CLIP embeddings and Visual Attention Redistribution, achieving 50–75% embedding compression with minimal accuracy loss across SQA-IMG, TextVQA, POPE, and MME.

Thierry Tambe · Tsachy Weissman · NVIDIA Research

B

Relational learning at scale

I contributed to RelBench v2, a benchmark and open-source repository for learning directly over interconnected relational tables. I worked on dataset integrations spanning TGB and TabArena sources, as well as unified loading and evaluation infrastructure; RelBench v2 expands the benchmark to 11 databases and 66 predictive tasks.

Jure Leskovec · Carlos Guestrin · Stanford

C

Block-parallel diffusion training

I helped develop and validate Block Parallelism, a distributed training method for long-context block diffusion models. It assigns independent target-block losses to GPU ranks and fuses block and context parallelism so corrupted-block state stays local while clean-prefix key/value state is sharded.

The system combines K/V-sharded attention, tiled cross-entropy, and DP-scoped ZeRO-2. In 16-H200 profiles, BP+CP speedups grow with context length, reaching 1.19× on Nemotron 14B at 512K and 1.78× on DiffusionGemma 26B-A4B at 128K.

Distributed training · Long context · Diffusion LLMs

D

Hybrid diffusion language models

I study Mamba-attention hybrids for block diffusion language modeling. Our partial-bidirectional design restricts reverse Mamba computation to the active denoising block, retaining local bidirectional mixing while making clean-prefix states exactly reusable across denoising steps.

Model architecture · Prefix caching · Long-context inference

E

Efficient model memory systems

At AMD, I developed head-level low-precision K/V-cache compression for open-source mixture-of-experts models, including GPT-OSS, Qwen, and DeepSeek. At NVIDIA, I characterized GPU memory-management and cache behavior across machine learning, graphics, and HPC workloads.

Quantization · K/V caches · GPU architecture

F

Multilevel cache replacement

My UT Austin honors thesis, advised by Calvin Lin, studied coordinated cache replacement across multiple hierarchy levels. I implemented and evaluated replacement policies in ChampSim against classical and oracle-inspired baselines.

Calvin Lin · UT Austin · Computer architecture
