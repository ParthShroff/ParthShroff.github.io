Machine learning systems + architecture

Parth Shroff

I work on efficient machine learning systems, spanning model architecture, distributed execution, and hardware-software co-design.

I completed an M.S. in Electrical & Computer Engineering (AI & Software/Hardware Systems) at Stanford, where my research spanned multimodal LLM inference and token compression, relational deep learning, and efficient distributed training and inference for diffusion language models. At UT Austin, I earned B.S. degrees in Computer Science, Electrical and Computer Engineering, and Mathematics as a Turing Scholar and Dean's Scholar, graduating with University Honors and CNS Distinction in Research.

My research spans efficient model training and inference, distributed and long-context training, diffusion language models, multimodal token compression, relational deep learning, quantization, K/V-cache optimization, GPU memory systems, computer architecture, and hardware-software co-design. I currently work at NVIDIA.
